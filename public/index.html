What we accept

Most computer hardware, working or not

Yes

  • Laptops, desktops, servers
  • Phones and tablets
  • Networking gear
  • Components: drives, RAM, GPUs, power supplies
  • Monitors (flat panel)
  • Keyboards, mice, cables, chargers
  • Broken laptops, desktops, and servers are welcome

No

  • CRT monitors and TVs
  • Printers
  • Kitchen and household appliances
  • Loose or swollen batteries

Not sure? Ask in the form. If we can't use it, we'll tell you where it should go instead.

What happens to your hardware

Repurposed, refurbished, or recycled — never landfilled

Repurposed.

Working and repairable gear goes into Hardware Club projects: home servers, test benches, rebuilds.

Refurbished and passed along.

Anything useful we don't need gets cleaned up and handed to someone who does.

Recycled properly.

What truly can't be reused goes to a certified electronics recycler, never the bin.

Your data.

Every drive is wiped or physically destroyed before anything else happens. If you want, we'll pull the drive and hand it back to you at drop-off.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is this a nonprofit? Is my donation tax-deductible?

No and no. Hardware Club is an independent project, not a registered nonprofit, so donations aren't tax-deductible. What you get instead is a straight answer about where your hardware goes.

What do you do with my data?

Every drive is wiped or physically destroyed before anything else happens. If you'd rather keep it, we can hand the drive back to you at drop-off.

Can I drop it off? Where?

Yes, drop-off is available in Metro Detroit. The exact spot is arranged by email after you submit the form.

Do you pick up?

Pickup isn't promised. If drop-off doesn't work for you, we'll figure something out together.

Do you take broken stuff?

Yes. Broken laptops, desktops, and servers are welcome.

What don't you take?

CRT monitors and TVs, printers, kitchen and household appliances, and loose or swollen batteries.

Who runs this, and why?

A small group of hardware enthusiasts in Metro Detroit who started Hardware Club because too much good hardware gets thrown away.

Can I get the drive back?

Yes. Just say so in the form or mention it at drop-off.

About

A small group of hardware enthusiasts

We're a small group of hardware enthusiasts in Metro Detroit. We started Hardware Club because too much perfectly good hardware ends up in the trash. Some of what you give us becomes part of our projects, some gets fixed up and passed along, and the rest is recycled the right way. Either way it doesn't end up in a landfill, and your data never leaves our hands.
