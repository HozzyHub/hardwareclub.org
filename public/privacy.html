Privacy

This page explains what happens when you use the donation form on this site. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

When you submit the form, we store your name, email, phone (if you give it), city or ZIP, a description of your hardware, and the choices you make in the form (category, quantity, whether it powers on, drop-off preference, and whether you want your drive back). We also record the date, your country (from your network), and a truncated browser identifier, so we can tell real submissions from spam.

Why we collect it

We need this information to reply to you, arrange a drop-off or handoff, and understand roughly what we're picking up.

How long we keep it

We keep submission data until the handoff is complete, or for 12 months, whichever comes first.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies.

Spam protection

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to keep the form free of automated spam. Turnstile may set its own cookies as part of that check; that's Cloudflare's, not ours. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Contact

Questions about this page or your data: hello@hardwareclub.org.
