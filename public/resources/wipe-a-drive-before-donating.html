How to wipe a drive before you donate it

Deleting files or emptying the trash does not erase anything. Here is what actually does, by drive type.

Step 0: back up what you want to keep

Wiping is permanent. Copy photos, documents, browser bookmarks and password-manager exports to an external drive or cloud account first, and check the copy opens.

Step 1: sign out and deauthorize

  • Apple devices: turn off Find My and sign out of your Apple ID. A device still tied to your account (Activation Lock) is very hard for anyone else to use.
  • Android and Chromebooks: remove your Google account before resetting, otherwise Factory Reset Protection can lock the next owner out.
  • Windows: sign out of your Microsoft account and deauthorize paid software (Adobe, music libraries, games) that limits installs.
  • Remove SIM and memory cards, and remove any BIOS/UEFI or firmware password.

Step 2: pick the method for your drive

DriveGood enough for donatingExtra careful
Hard disk (HDD, spinning)One full overwrite pass with zeros, using the built-in reset with "clean the drive", or a tool such as the open-source nwipe.Physical destruction if the drive held especially sensitive data or no longer works.
SSD / NVMeTurn on full-disk encryption, then reset the device (the encryption key is discarded, so what's left is unreadable), or run the maker's secure-erase tool.Firmware "sanitize" or "secure erase" commands. Avoid multi-pass overwrite tools: they don't reliably reach every cell on an SSD.
Phone / tabletSign out of accounts, then "Erase all content and settings" or a factory reset. These devices encrypt storage by default.Encrypt first (older Android), then reset.
Won't power on or can't be readPull the drive and keep it, or hand it to us separately.Physical destruction.

The reference for this is NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1, which describes "clear", "purge" and "destroy" levels. For ordinary personal data, "clear" (a full overwrite or an encrypted reset) is the sensible level.

Step 3: check it worked

After the reset, the device should boot to a first-time setup screen with no trace of your account. If you wiped a drive with a tool, try to open it: it should show as empty or unformatted.

Prefer to keep the drive?

If you'd rather not trust any software, take the drive out and keep it. Hardware Club will take the machine without it, and you can say so on the donation form. Tell us you want your drive back and we'll hand it over at drop-off.
