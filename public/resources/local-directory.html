Where to take it in Metro Detroit

Programs and hours change, so this directory links to official locators and local governments instead of listing addresses that go stale.

Find a drop-off

  • Earth911 recycling search Search by item and ZIP code for nearby recyclers.
  • Call2Recycle battery locator Free battery drop-off locations. Call ahead about damaged batteries.
  • EPA: electronics donation and recycling Overview of donating and recycling electronics, plus manufacturer take-back programs.

Local and state government

Counties and cities run household hazardous waste days, e-waste events and drop-off sites. Search each site for "electronics", "e-waste" or "household hazardous waste".

  • Wayne County
  • Oakland County
  • Macomb County
  • City of Detroit
  • Michigan EGLE (state environment department) Statewide rules and recycling program information.

Repair and know-how

  • iFixit Free repair guides and teardowns for phones, laptops and more.
  • National Digital Inclusion Alliance Directory of groups working on affordable devices and digital skills.

Manufacturer take-back

Many computer and phone makers run mail-in or in-store take-back programs. Search the maker's site for "recycling" or "trade-in", and wipe your data first (see the wipe guide).

Know a good local program? Email hello@hardwareclub.org with the name and link and we'll consider adding it. Hardware Club isn't affiliated with, and doesn't endorse or guarantee, the organizations linked here.
