Get a laptop or desktop ready to donate

Ten minutes of prep makes a donated computer much easier to put back to work.

Before you hand it over

  1. Back up anything you want to keep.
  2. Sign out and wipe. Follow the wipe guide, or tell us you want your drive back.
  3. Find the charger. A working power adapter is often the difference between a usable laptop and a parts machine. Check you have the right one for that model.
  4. Test it. Does it power on? Does it hold a charge? Does the screen, keyboard, trackpad and Wi-Fi work? Jot down what doesn't.
  5. Note the specs. Make, model, memory, storage and roughly how old it is. The label on the bottom or the "About" page has most of it.
  6. Remove personal items: stickers with your name, asset tags, adhesive residue, and anything stored inside the case.
  7. Pack it safely. A padded bag or a box with something soft around it is plenty. Keep the battery in the laptop unless it is swollen (see the battery guide).

What to tell us in the donation form

  • What it is and roughly how many you have.
  • Whether it powers on, and anything that's broken. Broken laptops, desktops and servers are welcome.
  • Whether you want a drive returned to you.

The printable donation checklist and inventory spreadsheet help if you're clearing out an office or a closet full of gear.

What we can't take

CRT monitors and TVs, printers, kitchen and household appliances, and loose or swollen batteries. The local directory lists where those can go instead.
